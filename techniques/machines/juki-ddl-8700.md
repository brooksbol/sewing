# Juki DDL-8700 — primary straight-stitch machine

**Current inventory confirmed directly 2026-11-04:** owned and used for approximately **99% of all stitching**. Industrial single-needle straight-lockstitch machine. This supersedes earlier shopping-history records that it was merely considered.

## Machine and supplies (historical shirting thread, supplied 2026-11-04)

- Needle system recorded: **DBx1 / 16×257**; current needle during fine-shirting trial: **new Organ DBx1 80/12**.
- High-shank industrial screw-mounted presser-foot system.
- Industrial metal bobbin, often described as L-style; **Juki part 229-32909** reported in source but verify compatibility before ordering.
- Oil specified in source: **Juki New Defrix Oil No. 1, ISO VG7**; confirm machine manual.
- Straight stitch only; **no built-in buttonholes**.
- Drop feed, not integrated walking/compound feed; manufacturer's target primarily light/medium fabric. Ownership does not establish heavy multilayer capacity.

## Fine broadcloth / dress shirting — actual reported trial

- Cloth: thin cotton broadcloth.
- Thread: Aurifil **50 wt cotton** under evaluation.
- Needle: Organ DBx1 **80/12**.
- Stitch length approximately **2.7 mm**.
- Initial puckering resolved by **lowering presser-foot pressure**.
- Upper-thread tension increased when small underside marks appeared; bobbin tension subsequently loosened slightly. Underside became cleaner but upper stitch looked almost continuous; reducing upper tension made underside marks recur.
- **Unresolved:** proper upper/bobbin tension balance and stitch appearance. Underside marks could be loose loops *or* visible lock points. Appearance alone cannot establish diagnosis.
- Suggested diagnostic *not recorded as completed*: doubled broadcloth, contrasting top/bobbin threads, fixed 2.7 mm length, minimum reliable foot pressure, measure actual stitch spacing over 25 mm, adjust upper tension in small steps without repeatedly changing bobbin.

The machine was reported to sew heavier twill with larger thread successfully before this fine-broadcloth tuning. Do not substitute this shirt setup for the heavier-thread/2.8–3 mm canvas settings recorded in the barn coat project without testing.

## Shirt construction reference

- Preferred **collar edge-stitch starting offset**: about 1/8 in (~3 mm), not a universal required standard; industrial high-shank guide/compensating foot was discussed, not confirmed purchased.
- Fine Aurifil 50 wt cotton offers a matte/traditional appearance, while fine industrial polyester/core-spun may offer better strength and consistency; a direct comparison was proposed, not completed.
- Candidate needle ranges discussed: DBx1 70/10 for especially fine cloth, 80/12 for general fine broadcloth, 90/14 for heavier Oxford, 100/16 for twill/canvas. Fabric/thread-specific testing is required.
- Buttonhole operations currently belong to the [Brother CS-6000i](brother-cs-6000i.md). See [buttonhole notes](../buttonholes.md).
