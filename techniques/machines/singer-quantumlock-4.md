# Singer Quantumlock 4 — serger

**Identity:** Singer Quantumlock 4; identification plate reads *Model Series 14U*. Previous discussion associated the 14U286B family, but the plate alone does **not** confirm that exact subtype. The remembered “2054” was discussed as a possible needle-system number, not established as the model number.

## Earlier failure and recovery (historical discussion)

The 2026-11-04 supplied summary reports a machine thought to be approximately 1987 vintage and refers to it as a **14U286B**. Later identification evidence confirms only the plate wording **Model Series 14U**; treat the subtype as unverified.

Previously observed: broken needles (twice), loud clicking and concern about looper interference/timing. The needles were reportedly checked for correct type/installation. Repair research included looper alignment, a 2007 Laura's Blog serger timing article and a printable checklist. **No actual timing adjustment is documented.**

The user reported recovery after opening panels, removing threads/needles, cleaning away debris, lubricating according to the manual, rethreading, and performing stitch tests. The historical procedure included brake cleaner and an outdoor leaf blower. **Do not repeat that as recommended service practice:** brake cleaner can harm plastics, coatings, seals and electrical components; use machine-safe cleaning and manufacturer maintenance directions. Exact original fault was never established. Subsequent status reported operational and better than before.

The thread named upper knife **412585** and lower knife **412749** as likely candidates; **compatibility is unverified** and should be checked against exact machine identification before ordering. Singer/Organ needles and Honeysew aftermarket knives were discussed, not necessarily purchased.

## Accepted medium canvas twill trial (before 2026-11-04)

- Access: remove free-arm cover to expose the easy stitch-width dial; knife screw adjustment was not necessary.
- Overlock width: approximately **5 mm**.
- Upper and lower looper tensions: each backed off roughly **one dial point** from previous setup.
- Needle tensions: unchanged.
- Stitch length: two samples tried; **shorter-length sample (right side of comparison photo)** was preferred and declared satisfactory. Numeric stitch-length setting not recorded.
- Fabric: medium canvas twill, described as somewhat lighter than average denim and heavier than Oxford cloth.
- Outcome: flat, adequately covering edge; maker wanted no further adjustment.
- Scope: a **tested starting baseline for similar medium/heavy wovens**, not a universal setting. Retest on actual shell cloth and thick intersections.

The barn coat shell currently has intentionally unenclosed plain seam allowances awaiting the lining, so this serger setting is a **general workshop reference**, not part of that coat's mandated construction.
