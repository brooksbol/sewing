# Temporary sewing workspace

**Source:** historical sewing/workwear discussion summarized 2026-11-04; configuration is a plan, not evidence of a built sewing workshop.

## Constraints

Two possible locations: basement for smaller work, and barn for temporary larger-scale cutting, sewing, pressing and hardware operations.

Barn was described as offering roughly **20 × 25 ft** deployable working area and substantial existing electrical circuits. Its primary use is equipment/machinery storage, so sewing infrastructure must fold away or be mobile and leave no permanent occupied floor space.

Priorities: fast setup/teardown, portable sewing machines, collapsible/mobile cutting and pressing surfaces, organized compact storage, and room for large canvas/tarp workpieces when deployed.

The potential two-location workflow is a major factor in the Sailrite LS-1 preference over an industrial table machine. No final bench/cutting layout or purchase is recorded.
