# Fabric behavior and working notes

## Heavy sale canvas — Hobby Lobby

**Observed 2026-11-04 in barn coat prototype:** Relatively heavy canvas used for the first Aldrich overgarment toile. Less desirable in hand/quality than the military twill reserved for the final coat, but valuable for rehearsing bulky seam assembly, structural stitching, topstitching, and machine pressure settings.

A lighter muslin toile is planned for subsequent body/sleeve fitting; muslin will not necessarily reproduce the heavier shell's drape or cap-easing behavior.

See [barn coat construction](../projects/barn-coat/construction.md).

## Military twill — planned barn coat shell

**On hand; final garment not yet cut/sewn (2026-11-04).** Preferred to the heavy prototype canvas. The intended coat has soft but durable workwear structure. No definitive machine settings, shrinkage test, or seam performance have been recorded on this exact cloth.

## Medium canvas twill — serger sample

A sample described as lighter than ordinary denim but heavier than Oxford cloth was used for a Singer Quantumlock 4 overlock adjustment. The resulting stitch sample was accepted. See [serger settings](machines/singer-quantumlock-4.md).

## General recording discipline

When new fabric observations arrive, preserve fiber content when known, weave, approximate weight, source, wash/pre-shrink treatment, needle/thread/foot settings, pressing/feeding behavior, fraying, seam bulk, and actual tested outcome. Never infer these from a store name alone.
