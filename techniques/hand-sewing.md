# Hand sewing, basting, and tailor's marks

**Source:** retrospective sewing/workwear thread summary supplied 2026-11-04.

## Working practice and supplies

- **Owned thread:** Yokota Daruma 40/2 cotton, primarily intended for temporary muslin assembly, tailor's tacks, fitting marks and placement guides.
- **Preferred needle type under consideration:** long, slender milliners/straw needles for running basting and tailor's marks. Do not mark as purchased.
- **Alternative discussed:** sturdier sashiko needles for heavy decorative running stitches or canvas.
- **Brands discussed, not established inventory:** John James, Tulip Hiroshima, Olympus.

Needle starting ranges discussed (manufacturer sizing differs): milliners **6–8 for muslin**, **5–6 for lighter canvas**, **3–5 for heavier layered canvas**. With Daruma 40/2 in muslin, **6–8** was favored. Check threading, penetration and fabric damage rather than treating the ranges as fixed.

Applications: prototyping, pocket/dart placement, temporary seams, marking/transfer, and occasional canvas basting. No documented finalized needle purchase or test outcome.
