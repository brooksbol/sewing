# Hardware installation

## Grommets — existing experiments

**Source:** retrospective conversation summary supplied 2026-11-04.

An inexpensive hammer-and-mandrel kit is **owned** and has been used on reinforced canvas. Some installed grommets allowed fabric to slip out even when the mandrel was fully seated.

**Possible explanations, not established:** hole size, grommet quality, barrel rollover/length, compression, and reinforcement. A seated mandrel is not proof of a secure grommet crimp.

**Proposed workshop direction, not purchased:** professional manual lever press with dies matched to size/barrel and good-quality grommets; potential sources discussed include C.S. Osborne, Stimpson, KAMsnaps and GoldStar Tool. Screw, electric and pneumatic presses were also compared. Intended division of labor: precision press in workshop; existing inexpensive kit for field emergency repairs in MOLLE setup.

No exact grommet size, die set, fabric-thickness specification, or successful press trial has been recorded.
