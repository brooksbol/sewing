## Source-of-truth convention

- **Google Drive:** working source files, measurement sheets, drafting documents, photographs, and other bulky/reference artifacts.
- **GitHub:** concise curated sewing project memory, person-specific logs (only with deliberate privacy approval), equipment and reusable technique knowledge; link to Drive material instead of duplicating it.
- Do not treat the source file names as proof of their internal contents or accuracy.
- Do not automatically copy personal measurements or copyrighted reference text from Drive into this **public GitHub repository**.
- Changes in Drive are not automatically synchronized to GitHub. Read current Drive material when needed for a particular task.
